## Submission Requirements

* If your paper has been accepted to the main conference (oral, poster, findings, TACL/CL), you are required to submit a pre-recorded video presentation, a poster PDF (if your paper is a poster) and presentation slides (if your paper is an oral)
* In case you should switch from in-person oral presentation to virtual presentation, please note that submitting a poster PDF is optional but highly recommended.
* All papers presented virtually need to follow the same requirements as posters as described in slide 3.
* If you are presenting a demo, a student research workshop paper or industry track paper, please see the requirements on slide 3
* All pre-recorded presentations, posters and slides must be uploaded to Underline by March 30, 2025 through a Speaker Form detailed on slide 4.
* On the following pages you can also find a simple set of guidelines on how to record your presentation: [Main Conference Presenters](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1cc-6uRTxs6V6wxC5ggWKi0ww11dFoP0VeVtwZKnkpaA/edit#slide=id.g136d1b54704_0_241).
* If you need help or have questions please email us at [naacl2025@underline.io](mailto:naacl2025@underline.io)
