Following the name change of the NAACL chapter (see [https://naacl.org/posts/2024-10-24-Name-Change-Announcement](https://naacl.org/posts/2024-10-24-Name-Change-Announcement) for details), we will also be changing the full name of the conference. We are now the 2025 Annual Conference of the Nations of the Americas Chapter of the Association for Computational Linguistics, still abbreviated as NAACL 2025. We’re thrilled to take this small step toward better inclusivity of all of the Americas!

Please be patient as we propagate this change across the many surfaces where the conference operates.

Thanks, 

NAACL 2025 Organizing Committee
